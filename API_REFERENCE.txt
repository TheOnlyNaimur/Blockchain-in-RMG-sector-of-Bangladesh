6. RECORDS (MongoDB)  —  /api/records
================================================================
All blockchain transactions are saved to MongoDB with:
  - rawData        : plain business payload
  - contractFeedback : on-chain receipt (txHash, blockNumber, blockHash, from, to, gasUsed, status)
  - dataHash       : SHA-256 of rawData for tamper detection

------------------------------------------------------------

GET /api/records
  Desc   : List all records (paginated + filterable)
  Query  : ?type=SELLER_REGISTERED&page=1&limit=20
  Body   : (none)
  Response: {
              "success": true,
              "total": 5,
              "page": 1,
              "limit": 20,
              "totalPages": 1,
              "data": [
                {

                  "recordType": "BUYER_REGISTERED",

================================================================
FULL SUPPLY CHAIN FLOW (Step by Step)
================================================================

Step 1   POST /api/sellers/register        (seller account)
Step 2   POST /api/sellers/approve         (server — certifier auto-signs)
Step 3   POST /api/buyers/register         (buyer account)
Step 4   POST /api/orders                  (seller creates order for buyer)
Step 5   POST /api/orders/:id/accept       (buyer accepts)
Step 6   POST /api/batches                 (seller records production batch)
Step 7   POST /api/batches/:id/quality     (server — quality checker auto-signs)
Step 8   POST /api/shipments               (seller requests shipment)
Step 9   POST /api/shipments/:id/doc       (server — freight forwarder auto-signs)
Step 10  POST /api/shipments/:id/export-verify  (server — export customs auto-signs)
Step 11  POST /api/shipments/:id/import-verify  (server — import customs auto-signs)
Step 12  POST /api/orders/:id/pay          (buyer pays seller)

Every step automatically saves rawData + contractFeedback to MongoDB
with a SHA-256 integrity hash.
